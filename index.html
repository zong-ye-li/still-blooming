My flower

A private place for the moments,
colours and words that are yours.

Return to a moment.

Sign in to your private space and choose a saved timepoint. Explore its orchid in 2D or 3D, rotate it, look closer, or turn its motion off.

Shortcuts take you straight to your note, colour and sharing choices. Earlier timepoints stay available.

How you enter your space

An invitation lets you activate your account. After that, return with your orchid sign-in name and password.

A small pause.

Tap your flower to reveal a short reflection. Come back for another thought, at your own pace.

Words, just for you.

Save a private note for this moment. With your permission, an AI conversation can draw on your message and saved note.

A colour that feels like you.

Pick a palette, choose any colour, or enter a HEX value. Save it with your flower and return to earlier saved colours.

Take your flower with you.

Create a phone wallpaper with your flower and a few words. Preview it, then download it. Your message stays on your device.

Share your nectar.

Once your note is saved, open Share your nectar. Write the words you want others to see, then choose Review & share.

After approval, your flower, chosen colour and nectar appear together in the garden.

Public nectar and anonymous replies are reviewed by AI before delivery. Your measurements and conversation stay private; your note is only shared if you copy its words into your nectar.

See where your words go

Make the space feel like yours.

Theme colour sets the tone of the logo and garden independently of your saved flower colour.

Try a colour on this guide

A little company.

A shared garden brings together flowers, reflections and encouragement.

Ten flowers appear in each selection. Choose one to spend a moment with its nectar, or use Reload Garden to discover another selection. Motion can be switched off.

Stay with someone’s words.

Open a flower to read its nectar. Send a heart to let its author know the words reached you.

Send a little kindness back.

If the author accepts replies, write an anonymous message. Review it, consent to AI review and send it to that author.

Keep the kindness.

In My flower, choose whether to receive replies to your nectar. Keep a message, discard it, or send a heart back.

Sent replies are shown separately. A heart is the only response you can send back; there is no direct chat between mothers.

The institution desk.

A new timepoint, prepared with care.

From a care record to a private flower.

An authorised care partner creates an invitation. Once the mother activates her account, staff can record a timepoint under the prior care agreement.

Choose the mother, care stage and date. Record only measurements that are available and authorised; leave the others blank.

Record what is available.

Enter measurements with their units. Paired readings and test context help keep the record consistent.

Leave room for what is missing.

Body measurements and weight context have their own fields. Expand the recording context for profile details.

Check, then create.

  1. Preview the entry.Check each measurement and see what is not provided.
  2. Confirm & save.Save a new private timepoint to the mother’s library.
  3. Return to the flower.The flower image is prepared first, with the 3D view following.
